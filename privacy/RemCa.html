RemCa Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

RemCa is an application that allows users to view the camera feed of one Android device from another Android device on the same local network. RemCa does not send video streams or authentication credentials to any external server operated by the developer as part of its core functionality.

1. Information Used by RemCa

RemCa may use the following information on the device in order to provide its functionality:

  • Camera video
  • Local network information required to discover and connect to devices on the same LAN
  • An app-generated device ID used to identify RemCa installations
  • Display names, identifiers, and authentication information for paired devices
  • Connection status, communication status, camera-facing state, and other operational information

2. Camera Video

Camera video captured by RemCa is transmitted directly to a Viewer device selected by the user on the same local network using WebRTC.

As part of its core functionality, RemCa does not upload camera video to any cloud service, external storage service, or external video streaming service operated by the developer.

3. Local Network Communication

RemCa communicates over the local network in order to discover devices, perform pairing and authentication, transfer video, and send camera control commands.

RemCa uses mDNS / DNS-SD (Android NSD) for device discovery, local TCP connections for authentication and signaling, and WebRTC for video transport.

4. Pairing Information

RemCa stores information about paired devices on the device so that the devices can securely authenticate each other during future connections.

Long-term pairing keys are encrypted using an app-local key protected by Android Keystore and are stored only in the app's local private storage.

Device IDs, encrypted pairing keys, and related pairing information are excluded from normal Android backup and device-transfer backups.

5. External Services

RemCa's core functionality does not use Firebase, an external signaling server, or external STUN/TURN servers.

RemCa does not send camera video, pairing keys, session keys, authentication proofs, or other sensitive authentication data to any external server operated by the developer.

6. Android Permissions

RemCa may request or use the following permissions in order to provide its features:

  • Camera: To capture video on a Camera device
  • Network-related permissions: To discover and connect to devices on the same LAN
  • Foreground service: To keep the Camera device available for connections and streaming
  • Notifications: To display the state of the Camera foreground service
  • Wake Lock: To help keep active connection processing running reliably when needed

7. Sharing with Third Parties

Except where required by law, the developer does not sell or provide RemCa authentication data or app-local data to third parties.

8. Data Deletion

Users can remove registered Cameras or Viewers from within the app to delete the related pairing information.

Removing a Viewer from the Camera device revokes that Viewer's access to the Camera.

Uninstalling RemCa removes data stored in the app's local storage in accordance with the normal behavior of the Android operating system.

9. Security

RemCa uses cryptographic authentication and encrypted communication mechanisms to protect pairing, authentication, signaling, and video communication between devices.

However, no method of electronic communication or device storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

RemCa is not designed for the purpose of collecting personal information from children of a specific age group.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app functionality changes, legal requirements change, or other updates become necessary. Material changes may be announced on this page or within the app.
