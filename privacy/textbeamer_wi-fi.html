Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TextBeamer Wi-Fi ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

TextBeamer Wi-Fi is designed to transfer user-selected text from an Android device to a web browser on a computer connected to the same local network.

1. Information We Collect

TextBeamer Wi-Fi does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information on external servers.

The App does not operate a cloud service for storing your transferred text.

The App may temporarily process the following information on your device:

  • Text that you explicitly choose to send to your computer.
  • Clipboard text when you explicitly open the Beam interface or otherwise request the App to use clipboard content.
  • Local network information required to communicate with your computer, such as the device's local IP address.
  • A locally generated pairing code and session credentials used to connect your browser to the App.
  • A user-defined name for the paired computer or browser profile.

2. Clipboard Data

TextBeamer Wi-Fi does not continuously monitor your clipboard.

Clipboard content is accessed only when you perform an action that requires the App to obtain text, such as opening the Beam interface or using a paste-related function.

Clipboard text used for a Beam operation is processed in memory and is not intentionally stored as a permanent text history by the App.

3. Text You Send

Text that you choose to Beam is transmitted from your Android device to the connected web browser over your local network.

The App does not intentionally upload this text to a developer-operated cloud server.

Text waiting to be sent or acknowledged may be temporarily kept in memory while the App is running. It is not intended to be stored as a permanent history.

4. Local Network Communication

TextBeamer Wi-Fi operates a local web service on your Android device so that a browser on your computer can connect to it.

The App may therefore request permissions required by Android to access and communicate with devices on your local network.

The local connection is intended to be used on a trusted private network, such as your home or workplace network.

The MVP version of TextBeamer Wi-Fi may use unencrypted HTTP and WebSocket communication on the local network. Because of this, you should not use the App to transmit highly sensitive information such as passwords, private keys, recovery phrases, or other confidential credentials.

5. Pairing and Session Information

The App generates pairing and session information to prevent arbitrary browsers from connecting to the text receiver.

Session verification information may be stored locally on your Android device so that a previously paired browser can reconnect.

The browser may store a session cookie used for authentication with the local Android receiver.

These credentials are used only for the local connection between your Android device and the paired browser.

6. Data Storage

TextBeamer Wi-Fi is designed not to maintain a permanent history of the text you Beam.

The App may store limited configuration information locally, including:

  • Paired browser or computer registration information.
  • Session verification information.
  • The user-defined paired computer name.
  • App preferences, such as Floating Beam settings and position.

Beam text, clipboard text, pairing codes, pending Beam messages, and temporary pairing sessions are not intended to be permanently stored.

7. Advertising and Analytics

TextBeamer Wi-Fi does not use advertising services.

The App does not intentionally use third-party analytics services to collect your Beam text or clipboard content.

8. Internet and Cloud Services

TextBeamer Wi-Fi is designed to perform its core text-transfer functionality without a developer-operated cloud service.

The App's core Beam functionality is intended to work between devices on the same local network.

9. Permissions

Depending on your Android version and the features you enable, TextBeamer Wi-Fi may request permissions related to:

  • Local network and internet/network communication.
  • Foreground service operation.
  • Notifications used to indicate that the local receiver is running.
  • Displaying the optional Floating Beam control over other apps.

These permissions are used to provide the corresponding App features.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Text you Beam is transmitted to the browser that you connect to the App on your local network. The App does not intentionally send Beam text to advertising networks or developer-operated cloud storage.

11. Children's Privacy

TextBeamer Wi-Fi is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

The App does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information from children through a developer-operated server.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures in the App design to limit unauthorized access, including local pairing and session authentication.

However, no method of electronic communication is completely secure. In particular, local HTTP or WebSocket traffic may be visible to other parties with sufficient access to the same network.

We recommend using TextBeamer Wi-Fi only on networks that you trust.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's functionality or privacy practices change.

When this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.
