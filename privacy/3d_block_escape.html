Privacy Policy

App Name: 3D Block Escape

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 3D Block Escape handles user information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information Collection

3D Block Escape does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on external servers. No account registration is required to use the app.

The app stores game progress, such as unlocked levels, locally on your device. This information is used only to maintain your game progress.

Game progress is not transmitted to our servers.

3. Advertising

The current version of 3D Block Escape does not display advertisements or use advertising SDKs.

Advertisements may be introduced in future versions. If advertising is added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

4. Third-Party Services

The current version of the app does not intentionally share personal information with third-party services.

5. Internet Connection

The game is designed to work offline and does not require an internet connection for gameplay.

6. Children's Privacy

3D Block Escape does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require users to provide personal information to play.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress is stored locally on your device. You can delete locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

8. Security

Because the app does not transmit gameplay data to external servers, game progress remains stored locally on your device.
